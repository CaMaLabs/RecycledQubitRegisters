# Prospective Dark Star N33 public-only adaptive QR-routing validation

Research lead: Chase Lunsford / CaMaLabs. Date: 2026-10-29. **CPU-only; zero real QPU or IBM free/paid Runtime.** Follow-up to the [original N32 middle-separation PTP result](DARK_STAR_N32_SEPARATION_VS_BRENT_RESULT.md), where PTP Q/B won 5/8 (strict PTP-specific primary failed), near ordinary Fermat won 8/8, and far QR scanning lost 8/8. This protocol is frozen before any new N33 candidate is ranked/scored. No prior N32 results are rebranded as a new holdout.

## A. Public-only adaptive routing hypothesis (N is sole target input)

Predeclare a predictor/online-probe strategy **A**, using only N and computations on N, never p,q, true midpoint displacement, construction stratum, or quantum order:

1. Check public divisibility by 2,3,5,7 and stop on proper factor, then exactly 8 ordinary Fermat midpoint tests (x starting ceil(sqrt(N))). Count every operation and total time including failed checks.
2. When these fail, create **fresh per-call N-specific** QR masks modulo the same proven safe wheel `255255=3·5·7·11·13·17`. Scan precisely the 64 distinct *next* Fermat midpoint positions x=ceil(sqrt(N))+8 through +71 and count modular membership checks/accepted positions and every permitted exact integer square root. If any position yields valid factors, stop. This scan is a **real bounded factor-search probe**, not a claim that residue density reveals factor separation.
3. Public-only route predicate after the 64-position probe: **if ≤2 of the 64 midpoint x positions survive all six quadratic-residue masks, extend the QR window through offset +639 (overall 632 post-Fermat x positions) with cap 64 total exact square roots; otherwise skip extended QR.** Always allow a real factor from the pilot regardless of predicted density. The predeclared eligible count threshold is an exploratory cost-selectivity heuristic, not validated as predicting unknown factor distance.
4. If unresolved after QR pilot/extension, run the SAME fixed strong Brent rho reference as standalone B, identical restart settings `(c=1,seed=2,max1024 squarings); (c=3,seed=3,max512)`, then **complete wheel210 fallback**. No hidden factor labels/stratum changes, paid/free QPU, or post-hoc parameter tuning. Count the whole QR pilot, mask setup, conditional extended search, any failed Brent attempts and wheel divisions.
5. Comparator **B** is standalone Brent with 2,3,5,7 public checks and the exact same rho/wheel guarantee (no wasted Fermat or QR). Comparator **F** is 8 Fermat squares then B. Comparator **Q** is a fixed full safe QR scan from offset +8 through +639 (and its first eight plain Fermat checks), with same 64 square-root total cap then B: acts as a **nonadaptive full-QR portfolio**. Comparator **W** is complete wheel210. Comparator **L** is complete PTP LFK. Comparator **M** is ordinary Fermat up to offset +639, allowed to fail. All complete B/F/Q/A/W/L methods must return exactly correct factors on every synthetic N33 test. No condition reads true p/q/stratum.
6. Predictor's **accepted-position count among first 64** is measured online for every case that passes F8; record its numerical value even when it chooses to skip long QR. Report confusion vs the **post-validation** known separation labels, and record whether it actually saved runtime; don't claim predictive accuracy from a selected population or threshold picked on these cases.
7. QR masks should be charged **per algorithm invocation**; no warm cache free lunch after benchmarking B, Q, F. All six complete methods run **9 rotated interleaved repetitions** per public N, preserve all raw timings and operation counts; claim only descriptive microsecond Python comparisons, not asymptotic gains.

Public manifest contains only N, stratum **experiment metadata**, rank, N mod210, bit size; **NO factors, D numeric values, costs, fitted threshold or timings**. ## C. Fixed gates and negative controls

- **PRIMARY public adaptive routing advantage**: A must be faster than complete standalone strong Brent B in **≥15/24** original N33 cases **AND** faster than fixed full-QR portfolio Q in **≥15/24** cases, measured median wall time for the same N over nine interleaved repeats. Both thresholds mandatory; otherwise FAIL.
- **SECONDARY selective middle-stratum gate**: A must be faster than B in ≥6/8 **middle** cases and choose the extended QR stage in at least 4 of 8 middle cases, while skipping the extended QR in at least 6 of 8 far cases. This jointly tests public cost-selectivity; record all three values and fail if any fails.
- **Tertiary diagnostics**: successes against F (cheap Fermat control), W and L; empirical route confusion versus stratum labels (never available to the algorithm); percentage of total time spent on unproductive pilot/QR; exact sqrt savings and modulus membership. A wins over W/L alone cannot establish method-invention advantage; a win from Fermat near cases is not a PTP-specific win.
- All 24 factor recoveries correct for six complete algorithms, all 9 raw timings, all B budgets capped, nulls retained. Do not equate source CCX with CPU divisions/timing. No quantum computation at all.
- Report with true chronology (including failed CI/setup attempts) and commit original JSON+log milestone before any subsequent experiment. No security-scale factoring or general speedup claims.
