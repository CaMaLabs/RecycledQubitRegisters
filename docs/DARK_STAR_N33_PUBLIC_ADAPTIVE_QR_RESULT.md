## Algorithm actually tested

The public **A** strategy tests divisibility by small wheel primes; checks eight plain Fermat midpoints; then runs a real **64-position public QR midpoint probe** for x offsets +8 to +71 using six public prime quadratic-residue masks (wheel M255255). If those fail, its precommitted public *selectivity heuristic* extends QR through x offset +639 (up to 64 exact square roots in both QR scans combined) **iff ≤2 of the initial 64 candidates passed the public quadratic-residue mask**. If none finds the factors it invokes exactly the same **strong optimized Brent** restarts c=1 seed2 max1024 modular squarings, c=3 seed3 max512, and full conventional wheel210 fallback. Complete source construction, QR mask setup, scan costs, Fermat, failed prefixes, rho and fallback are included.

Seven separately timed methods: adaptive A; standalone strong Brent + wheel B; cheap Fermat8+Brent F; nonadaptive Fermat8 + full QR640+Brent Q; complete wheel W; complete PTP-LFK kernel L; bounded plain Fermat640 M (can return unresolved). All six complete methods A/B/F/Q/W/L recovered **24/24 exact proper factors**, without hidden p/q or true D in the route.

- **PRIMARY gate FAIL:** preregistration required **≥15/24** A>B *and* **≥15/24** A>Q. Actual **14/24** against Brent, only **2/24** against nonadaptive full QR. The median A/B time ratios were **0.0345** in near, **0.6757** in middle, **2.2541** in far. The near improvements came from plain eight-step Fermat, NOT any QR search.
- **SECONDARY joint selectivity gate FAIL:** middle A>B required ≥6/8, achieved **6/8**; middle extended QR must be selected ≥4/8, achieved **7/8**; far extended QR must be SKIPPED ≥6/8, achieved **0/8**. The cheap QR-survivor density rule **completely failed to reject the far cases**. All eight far originals were routed to the expensive extended QR scan before finally needing Brent.
- **Misclassification example:** `N=7,334,956,339`, validation-only middle D=85, had **three** public eligible midpoint positions in the 64-position probe. The threshold two caused A to **skip** extended QR, then run Brent; A/B median runtime ratio **1.337**. The fixed full QR control Q would have found the correct factors at actual x displacement 85. In contrast far N values frequently had **zero or one** eligible pilot positions, so the heuristic assigned them expensive QR scans which all failed.
- **No public factor-distance predictor established.** Filtering is an exact necessary mathematical condition for a *given x* to be a Fermat midpoint, but the number of candidate QR residues in a short local window does **not** reliably reveal how far away the first actual prime-pair midpoint lies. Sparse candidate residues alone are not evidence that successful factor recovery is nearby.
- Nine timed Python microbenchmark repetitions and a synthetic 33-bit cohort cannot establish device-level, cryptographic, algorithm-independent or asymptotic speed claims. We do not treat favorable synthetic stratum labels as inputs to unknown-integer factoring. No QPU, IBM Runtime, native CZ/depth or quantum arithmetic was invoked.

## Preserved positive insight and next falsifiable direction

The **original geometric/PTP residue pruning remains valid** and, on this independent N33 cohort, an extended QR scan can materially help when factors are moderately close: it recovered 7/8 middle cases under the adaptive rule and won against Brent in 6/8. **However this public pilot cannot decide when it is worth paying for QR.** It selected QR for all eight far cases and failed both preregistered decision gates.

The correct next effort is to avoid hidden factor-distance heuristics altogether: consider an **anytime, interleaved budget scheduler** with short Fermat, small bounded QR, incremental Brent, and progressively allocated resources controlled by **public computation and current search failure**, with a guaranteed complete wheel fallback. That would test whether *online task scheduling* can limit worst-case wasted QR costs without pretending to infer a secret p/q distance. Any new scheduler and thresholds must be preregistered and tested on a **new** independent cohort, against Brent alone and against the nonadaptive QR baseline from the beginning. An optimized standalone Fermat8+Brent must also remain a control. Do not promote a new result on the old N33 population as a prospective holdout.
